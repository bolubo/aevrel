# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

shardferry is a technical project for people with different backgrounds,
experience levels, employers, and opinions. We want participation to be
professional, direct, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Give reproducible evidence when reporting a problem.
- Accept correction and revise a position when the evidence changes.
- Respect confidentiality, credentials, customer data, and checkpoint data.
- Make space for newcomers who are trying to understand the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing private information, credentials, checkpoints, or customer data.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces where someone is representing shardferry.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the current maintainer listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). If the concern involves the maintainer, use
GitHub's private reporting channel for the repository.

Do not include sensitive material in a public issue. Maintainers will review
reports in confidence and may respond with a warning, content removal,
temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces. Good-faith
reporters will not be retaliated against.

This policy is informed by Contributor Covenant 2.1.
